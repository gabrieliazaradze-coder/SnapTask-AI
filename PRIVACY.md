# SnapTask AI — Privacy Notice

SnapTask AI is an early prototype. Its current plans are demo-generated in your browser and are not generated by a live AI service. The app stores your current plan and checklist progress in your browser's local storage; this information is not synced to a server by this prototype. Clearing browser data may erase it.

The installable web app may cache its app shell on your device for faster loading. It does not currently include accounts, cloud sync, analytics, or payment processing.

SnapTask Plus is a proposed future feature and is not for sale. No payment is collected.

Contact: use the project's GitHub repository to report privacy questions or issues.
