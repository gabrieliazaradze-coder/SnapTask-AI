# SnapTask AI — prototype privacy note

This prototype creates demo plans locally in your browser. Your latest prompt, task text, and completion checkboxes are saved in that browser's local storage so you can return to your plan on the same device and browser. They are not synced across devices or accounts. Clearing browser site data removes saved plans. Do not enter sensitive or confidential information.

The app is not yet connected to a real AI model or payment processor. The Plus pricing shown on the website is a proposed future price only; checkout is disabled and no payment is collected. This note is a prototype disclosure, not a substitute for a reviewed privacy policy before a commercial launch.
